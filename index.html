How to Use This Radio

  1. Type a station name in the Station box and press Enter to search, or use Down Arrow to choose a known station.
  2. Press Play to start the station.
  3. Press Play again to pause and resume playback.
  4. Press Stop to completely stop the current stream.
  5. Use Mute to mute or unmute the sound.
  6. Use the Volume control to change the volume.
  7. Search for additional stations by typing part of a station name and pressing Enter.
  8. Open Favourite Stations to select, add, or remove favourite stations.

Some Internet radio stations may occasionally be unavailable. A station can also change or discontinue its streaming address.
